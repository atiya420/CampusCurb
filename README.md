# CampusCurb 🍽️

**CampusCurb** is a smart campus food management platform designed to make college canteens more efficient, convenient, and sustainable.

It uses data-driven insights to **predict food demand, reduce food waste, and improve meal availability**. Students can explore available food options, while canteen staff can use demand insights to plan inventory and preparation more effectively.

## 🚀 Features

- 📊 **Demand Prediction** — Predicts food demand using historical data.
- 🍱 **Food Discovery** — Helps students explore available food options.
- 📉 **Waste Reduction** — Helps minimize overproduction and food waste.
- 📈 **Data Insights** — Provides useful insights for canteen management.
- ⚡ **Simple & Responsive UI** — Designed for a smooth campus experience.

## 🎯 Problem

College canteens often struggle with unpredictable food demand, leading to overproduction, food wastage, or shortages. Students may also have limited information about what's available.

## 💡 Solution

CampusCurb combines a simple student-facing interface with data-driven demand prediction to help canteens prepare the right amount of food while giving students a better dining experience.

**Less waste. Better planning. Smarter campuses.**

CampusCurb aims to transform traditional college canteens into smarter, data-driven food ecosystems.
